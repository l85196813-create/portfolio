02 · Formula Student · DynamiΣ PRC

A front wing built to survive the cones.

Structural design of the composite front wing for the Politecnico di Milano Formula Student prototype.

The problem

A Formula Student front wing has to be as light and stiff as possible to carry aerodynamic loads, yet strong enough to survive the hits it takes on track, especially against the cones that mark the course.

What I did

  • Designed the composite lamination of the wing.
  • Defined its internal architecture and the mounting system to the car.
  • Ran several simulations, including an explicit impact analysis to evaluate the strength ratio of the wing when hitting a cone at different speeds.
